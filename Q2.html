10 Reasons Why This Website Has Poor Design

Website I Chose

For this assignment, I chose the Bella De Soto website. This website was included in a 2026 article about bad website designs. After looking at the example, I found several reasons why its design could be improved.

Visit the website: Bella De Soto Website

10 Reasons Why the Website Is Bad

  1. Too Much Content: The website contains too much information on its homepage, making it difficult for visitors to focus on what is important.
  2. Poor Organization: The content does not appear to be organized in a clear way, making it harder to find information.
  3. Low-Quality Pictures: Some images appear to have poor quality, which makes the website look less professional.
  4. Too Much Scrolling: Visitors have to scroll both horizontally and vertically, making the website harder to use.
  5. Oversized Layout: The webpage takes up too much space and does not present its information in a convenient layout.
  6. Poor Visual Design: The different elements do not work together well, making the overall appearance less organized.
  7. Lack of Clear Sections: Related information should be grouped together instead of being spread throughout the page.
  8. Not User Friendly: Visitors may have trouble finding the information they are looking for because of the layout.
  9. Image Sizes: The large images take up unnecessary space and make the page harder to navigate.
  10. Needs a Responsive Design: The website should be better organized so that visitors can use it comfortably on computers, tablets, and mobile phones.

Conclusion

Overall, I believe the Bella De Soto website could improve by having a cleaner layout, better-quality images, and more organized information. A good website should be easy to understand and allow visitors to find what they need without difficulty. This example helped me understand why organization, appearance, and user experience are important when creating a website.
